# Adjudication Summary

> **DEMONSTRATION BASELINE — SYNTHETIC DATA — NOT CLIENT APPROVED**

The synthetic worst-case corpus contains 15 adjudication cases. Thirteen are adjudicated with confirmed ground truth, one remains disputed, and one remains in review. Each case records steward, source evidence, system output, expected class, action history, and audit event. The console supports confirm, correct, ambiguous, escalation to the responsible engineer, and dispute resolution. Only an authorized steward, engineer, reviewer, or admin may resolve a case; ordinary viewers cannot change ground truth.

The disputed and in-review cases remain visible as evidence gaps and are not silently converted into approval.
