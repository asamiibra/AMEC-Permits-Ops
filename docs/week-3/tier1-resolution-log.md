# Tier 1 Resolution Log

> **DEMONSTRATION BASELINE — SYNTHETIC DATA — NOT CLIENT APPROVED**

Tier 1 decisions cover field authority, attachment applicability, requirement dependencies, drawing metadata, municipality interaction, submission confirmation, data access, and professional responsibility. Resolved items retain their source, rationale, owner, and resolution date. Open items include attachment applicability, data access, and professional-responsibility boundaries. A fallback may be recorded, but it does not constitute client approval.
