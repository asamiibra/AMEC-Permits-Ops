# Spike Analysis v1

> **DEMONSTRATION BASELINE — SYNTHETIC DATA — NOT CLIENT APPROVED**

The analysis is intentionally separated into four dimensions:

- Classification: 15 documents; 13 correct, 1 incorrect, 1 abstained; agreement 13/15.
- Candidate extraction: 85 critical-field samples; 67 correct, 8 incorrect, 10 missing; agreement 67/85; 11 manually keyed and 15 human-corrected.
- Final control quality: 85/85 verified, zero false accepts; this is human-controlled quality, not autonomous quality.
- Effort/evidence: median verification 3.8 minutes per document, P90 7.4; evidence usability 8 GOOD, 3 USABLE, 3 POOR, 1 MISSING.

Failure modes include OCR unreadability, stamp occlusion, rotation, low resolution, Arabic segmentation, layout variation, field absence, and wrong classification. The synthetic profile is a decision aid and requires more evidence before contractual thresholds can be approved.
