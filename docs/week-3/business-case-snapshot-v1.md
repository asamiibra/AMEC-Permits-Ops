# Business Case Snapshot v1

> **DEMONSTRATION BASELINE — SYNTHETIC DATA — NOT CLIENT APPROVED**

The synthetic business baseline uses the Week 1 default case and records volume, applications, labor assumptions, rework, turnaround, cost, revenue, and a planning sensitivity. KPI targets are provisional synthetic targets only: cycle-time reduction, rework reduction, verification effort, exception rate, traceability, and first-pass readiness. No savings, ROI, or investment approval is represented as client fact.
