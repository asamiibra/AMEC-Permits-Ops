# Party-change revalidation

Material role, authorization, or contact changes mark open PreparationRevision records `NEEDS_REVALIDATION` and set the authority state likewise; locked/submitted/historical records are not rewritten. Active same-role assignments are superseded before the replacement assignment is recorded. The focused test replaces/extends the Consultant role and proves the open preparation revalidates. Full authority-specific cancellation workflows and obligation replacement workflows remain out of scope.
