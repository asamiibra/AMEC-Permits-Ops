# Commercial hardening

AMEC Fee, currency, payment terms, delivery duration, validity, inclusions, and exclusions remain Proposal transaction values. External/pass-through cost assumptions are separately typed with treatment, estimate, currency, body, rationale, and status; they are not folded into AMEC Fee or Contract truth.

Area and duration semantics remain explicit (`area_kind`, unit, and service-duration language). Safe default currency is QAR; validity and official commercial policy remain Owner-input seams.
