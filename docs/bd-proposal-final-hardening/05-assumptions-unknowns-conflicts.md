# Assumptions, unknowns, conflicts

Assumptions remain typed commercial/scoping rows. Unknowns are distinct unresolved facts. Conflicts retain both source/value sides and materiality. Material open unknowns/conflicts block Accept until a human acknowledgment is recorded.

Acknowledgment is deliberately not resolution: the target hash, actor, timestamp, and note are retained, and the projection exposes `acknowledged_is_not_resolved=true`.
