# Safety and authority boundaries

This preparation branch makes no application, Azure, SQL, deployment, Entra,
storage, Synology, or real-data mutation. It creates no migration, index,
retrieval table, cache, vector store, or external search resource.

The future commissioning harness may read synthetic runtime health and
synthetic fixture responses only. Retrieval remains read-only and citation
preserving. AI output remains advisory/draft context; it cannot approve,
submit, issue, price, release, stamp, or otherwise execute protected human
actions. Final Permit submission, Contract authority, Proposal acceptance, and
professional Engineering approval remain existing human/domain commands.

The separate Azure lane governs managed identity, SQL, deployment revision,
environment configuration, and rollback. No branch merge or cherry-pick is
performed in Step 4.
