# Assisted ergonomics measurement

`OperatorTaskTiming` records role, variant, task type, revision, duration, corrections, navigation count, evidence views, and evidence source. It does not log keystrokes, OTPs, or sensitive content. A small sample is labelled demonstration baseline rather than a contractual threshold.

The Week 11–12 synthetic demonstration records one baseline sample at 120,000 ms with three corrections and one optimized sample at 70,000 ms with one correction. These values are evidence of the measurement path and an observed improvement in the fixture only; they are not client-approved SLAs.
