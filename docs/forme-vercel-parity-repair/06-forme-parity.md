# FORME parity contract

The repair target is exactly 14 FORME masters: 7 Current and 7 Needs Review.
There is no invented Inactive FORME record. The three ProposalOps functional
masters remain Current, active, and resolver-eligible.

Needs Review records remain visible and openable with history and their exact
review notes, but normal current-master resolution excludes them through the
existing `needs_review=false` predicate. Current records remain subject to
existing module/purpose and current-version readiness rules.

Business/master-content parity is the target. Actual FORME binary storage on
Vercel remains uncertified; the local Samba acceptance remains historical
local evidence only.
