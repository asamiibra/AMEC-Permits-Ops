# ProposalOps / AMEC owner direction

This release makes ProposalOps the visible product name and AMEC Work the primary work surface. The visible business navigation is AMEC Work, Proposals & Contracts, Issues, Notifications, and Operating Guide.

The only demo personas are Owner, Engineering, and Business Development. Owner has full visibility. Business Development owns commercial, client, bid, quotation, and contract work. Engineering owns technical proposal preparation. All personas read from the same governed project, proposal, contract, task, issue, and evidence substrate.

Legacy `/permits` paths and internal role/API identifiers remain compatibility-only implementation details.
