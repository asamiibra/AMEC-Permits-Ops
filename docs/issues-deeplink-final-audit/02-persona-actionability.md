# Persona actionability

Business Development can act on Proposal Commercial and Contract issues. Proposal Technical, Permit Technical, and Authority issues are context-only for Business Development and do not expose Engineering-owned mutations.

Engineering can act on technical Proposal Preparation, Permit drawing review, and Authority comment disposition. Owner receives the meaningful union while backend capability checks remain authoritative.

The UI labels context-only destinations as context and uses safe navigation/read views. Backend checks deny Business Development attempts to mark Engineering preparation ready or confirm technical Permit sources.
