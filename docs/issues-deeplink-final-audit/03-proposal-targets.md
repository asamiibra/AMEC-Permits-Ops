# Proposal targets

Proposal Technical routes Business Development to Proposal Detail and Engineering to Proposal Preparation. The preparation view exposes technical fields only to Engineering/authorized roles; Business Development has no Ready for BD action or technical mutation.

Proposal Commercial routes Business Development to Proposal Detail with price, commercial context, stage, next action, and the focused Issue banner. Proposal Detail uses structured status and key-value cards, linked interactive Issues, cleaned source wording, and handoff history that matches the canonical record.
