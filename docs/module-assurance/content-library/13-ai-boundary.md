# AI Boundary

`POST /api/master-content/ai-assist` is fail-closed with `AI_ASSIST_NOT_ENABLED`; the Dashboard presents no enabled AI write path. Governed prefill is a controlled source/citation seam and does not grant AI authority over `AUTHORITY_ONLY` fields.

AI and Content Library are prohibited from approving, signing, stamping, releasing, submitting, deciding authority applicability, creating verified evidence, changing Project requirements, or creating professional engineering approval. Negative tests cover disabled AI, authority-only writes, stale versions, restricted sources, persona leakage and ambiguous resolution.
