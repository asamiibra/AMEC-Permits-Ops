# Stage 2 threshold evidence v0

No production acceptance thresholds are hardcoded in Week 2. The synthetic run provides baseline evidence for later threshold setting: perfect classification on the seeded labels, perfect candidate agreement on the measured labels, 17.65% manual-keyed and correction rates, 18-second median verification time, and known poor-evidence / wrong-class cases.

Thresholds require a confirmed acceptance corpus, adjudication protocol, representative language and scan-quality coverage, and client-approved risk tolerances. Candidate metrics must not be promoted to final-control metrics without verified-value sampling.
