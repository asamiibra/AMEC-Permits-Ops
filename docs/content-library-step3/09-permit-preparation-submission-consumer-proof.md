# Permit, preparation, and submission classification

Permit policy/rule lineage may point to Master Content as source authority and persists exact source IDs. Preparation and submission consume project/case evidence and package items, not reusable Master Content selection. Their DocumentVersion/FormInstance pointers remain transactional and context-bound. No blind Master Content integration was added.

Completion's reusable form endpoint is the exception: it now verifies the profile's exact FORM item/version before creating a case-bound FormInstance. Final authority submission remains human-controlled.
