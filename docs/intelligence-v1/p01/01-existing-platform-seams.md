## Must-not-change boundaries

Prompt 01 does not alter the above services, models, migrations, authorization policy, module commands, workflow semantics, storage providers, deployment configuration, or any business data. Later work must preserve candidate-vs-verified, review-vs-authority, eligibility-vs-execution, and currentness fail-closed behavior.
