# Permit terminology reconciliation

ProposalOps is the product and Proposals & Contracts is the commercial collection. Permit remains reserved for the actual downstream Permit domain: Permit Application, Permit Workspace, Municipality Preparation, Permit Submission, Authority Review, and Permit Findings. Legacy product labels such as PermitOps, Permit Workflow as the whole product, and Permits as the commercial collection are removed or rebranded.

The runtime surface uses explicit replacements for legacy branding only; it does not globally replace the word Permit.
