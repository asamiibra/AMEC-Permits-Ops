Finance/handover image: Invoice, Invoice Follow-up projection, Accountant/Generic Finance Handoff, invoice/no-invoice human decision, Project Handover, Handover Form/output/download reference, and client email draft are represented in E6. Client communication remains `HUMAN_SEND`. G.M. routing, authoritative Qatar/NFPA editions, DWF/CAD semantics, accounting truth, and signed owner decisions remain explicitly unresolved or safely bounded.
