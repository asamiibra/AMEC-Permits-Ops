# English Copy

The English Guide uses plain, operational language and the following hero:

> ProposalOps helps AMEC move work from tender and proposal through contract and permit — with one controlled record of sources, decisions, handoffs, and next actions.

The Guide defines Proceed as Proposal Intake having enough verified information to move into Engineering Proposal Preparation. It does not describe Proceed as save, commercial approval, Contract creation, or external submission.

The Guide uses only Owner, Business Development, and Engineering as normal user-facing roles. It describes Proposal → Contract and Contract → Permit as controlled lifecycle transitions while avoiding unsupported one-to-one promises.
