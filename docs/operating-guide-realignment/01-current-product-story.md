# Current Product Story

ProposalOps is the connected AMEC commercial-to-delivery workflow. A controlled record links tender and client evidence, Proposal intake, Engineering preparation, BD review, Contract, Contract handoff, Permit preparation, and Municipality / Authority follow-up.

The Guide explicitly distinguishes:

- AMEC Work: what needs attention now.
- Issues: what is blocking, wrong, or at risk.
- Notifications: what changed and what users should know.
- Administration: Owner configuration and system status.
- Inputs & Go-Live: what AMEC still needs to provide or configure.

The synthetic prototype explanation is factual: internal workflow, relationships, controls, and persistence are exercised as application behavior; client/government credentials, production data, live Synology, live email, and real government submission are not used.
