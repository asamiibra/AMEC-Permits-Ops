# Egyptian Arabic Copy

The Arabic Guide is a professional Egyptian-Arabic operating explanation, not a literal word-for-word translation. Product and business labels remain recognizable where that improves clarity: ProposalOps, AMEC, Proposal, Contract, Permit, Business Development, Engineering, Proceed, Project Reference, and Contract Reference.

The Arabic copy carries the same lifecycle, role boundaries, source/evidence meaning, human submission boundary, synthetic-mode qualification, and navigation destinations as English. Mixed identifiers and business labels are isolated with `bdi[dir="ltr"]`.
