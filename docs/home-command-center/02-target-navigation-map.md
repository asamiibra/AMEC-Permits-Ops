# Target navigation map

Primary navigation is now:

```text
HOME
  Home
BUSINESS FLOW
  Intake & Opportunity
  Contract & Mobilization
  Design & Technical Delivery
  Regulatory & Submissions
  Construction & Post-Approval
  Completion & As-Built
  Handover & Closeout
SYSTEM
  Admin
  Operating Guide
```

Owner/System Admin exposes the full seven-stage flow plus both system entries. Business Development sees Home, Intake & Opportunity, Contract & Mobilization, Regulatory & Submissions, Completion & As-Built, and Operating Guide. Engineering sees Home, Design & Technical Delivery, Regulatory & Submissions, Construction & Post-Approval, Completion & As-Built, Handover & Closeout, and Operating Guide.

AMEC Work, Finance, Content Library, Issues, and Notifications are intentionally absent from the primary sidebar but remain directly reachable.
