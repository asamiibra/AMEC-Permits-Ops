# Notifications experience

`/notifications` presents persona-specific awareness cards and event rows. The same event ID can produce different messages for Owner, Business Development, and Engineering. Read/acknowledge is an event operation only; it does not complete AMEC Work or close an Issue.

Delivery failure rate, failed deliveries, and fallback-recipient evidence are retained in the lower Delivery Diagnostics panel. They are not the primary Business Development or Engineering KPI cards.
