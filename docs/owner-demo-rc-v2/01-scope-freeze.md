# Scope Freeze

Frozen owner-demo scope: AMEC Work as the canonical homepage; Owner, Business Development, and Engineering role boundaries; owner Administration; Proposals & Contracts; source provenance and lineage; Issues and Notifications; permit lifecycle workspace and Stage 1 Confirm project & sources; human-only final submission boundary.

The release candidate does not reopen already-passing page realignments or restore superseded dashboard/language controls. Historical tests that expect the retired `Resume permit work` page, `Role` selector, or `.global-language-switch` are classified as obsolete assertions and are not release gates.
