# Human submission handoff runbook

1. Confirm the current PreparationRevision.
2. Confirm package version/hash and approval; reject stale package approval.
3. Confirm precheck clearance and no applicable blocking Finding.
4. Confirm portal draft reconciliation and current evidence.
5. Name the distinct Final Submitter and record role context.
6. Transfer the exact checklist; Final Submitter independently reviews the authority application.
7. The human submits outside PermitOps machine capability.
8. Capture submission attestation/evidence, then reread authority state where permitted.
9. Create/confirm SubmittedSnapshot and detect ExternalMutation.
10. If state/reference does not match, do not record `SUBMITTED_CONFIRMED`.

“I clicked submit” is insufficient evidence.
