# Contract Workspace

Every valid Contract has a stable owner workspace shell: overview, proposal origin, inputs, Client and contacts, commercial terms, Documents Needed, Deliverables, Contract Documents & Sources, configuration, revision/authority state, Project Activation, Billing context, and history. Empty optional collections remain honest and do not crash the detail route.

The six owner-sketch delta surfaces are present: Client Document, LPO, six explicit Client fields, source lineage, Documents Needed, Deliverables, Contract Documents & Sources, and explicit Accept Contract. The Contract detail browser suite passed on real PostgreSQL.
