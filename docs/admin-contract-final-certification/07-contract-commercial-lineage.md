# Contract Commercial Lineage

Contract Amount, Valuation Amount, Invoice Amount, Payment Condition, Billing Milestones, Project Duration, Project Start Date, and Construction Duration are separate facts. Contract terms and deliverable commitments remain Contract-owned; BillingPlan/BillingMilestone and Invoice records are downstream and are not silently created by Contract editing.

Field-level provenance is exposed for Client Name, Company, CR, Mobile, PIN, Email, Project Description, Amount, Payment Condition, Duration, Valuation, Detailed Works, Documents Needed, and Deliverables. PIN remains unresolved/safe rather than guessed.
