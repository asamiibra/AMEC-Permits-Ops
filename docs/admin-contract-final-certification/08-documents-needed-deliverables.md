# Documents Needed and Deliverables

Documents Needed use revision-scoped Contract input requirements with requiredness, source/policy metadata, evidence linkage, status, and readiness effect. They are not conflated with downstream RequirementInstance records. Deliverables use ContractDeliverableCommitment and remain distinct from EngineeringDeliverable records.

The UI supports honest empty/not-started states and the backend tests cover creation, read-back, readiness, and role boundaries. Final policy names, official checklists, and authoritative deliverable content remain owner-owned.
