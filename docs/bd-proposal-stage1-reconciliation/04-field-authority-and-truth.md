# Field authority and truth

External facts follow source → observation → human verification. Commercial Proposal content follows authorized authoring → Proposal revision → human Accept → immutable Accepted Proposal Revision. Regulatory scoping is intent only and never creates an AuthorityCase. Client Requested Scope is immutable/source-context semantics; AMEC Scope and Process of Work are Engineering/Proposal revision semantics. Client Budget is observed client input; AMEC Price is governed commercial content. Requested Timing is client context; AMEC Commercial Duration and Proposal Validity are separate commercial values.

AI assistance remains disabled. Human notes are retained as human-entered observations/context and require explicit verification or assumption acknowledgement before material use.
