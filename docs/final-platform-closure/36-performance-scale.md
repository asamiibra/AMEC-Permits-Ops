# Performance and scale

Handover-only synthetic scale evidence covered 200 projects, 400 services/packages/revisions, 8,000 items, 400 requirements/distributions/receipts, and 4,000 punches. List-all median was 12.33 ms and p95 152.29 ms; project-list median was 2.78 ms and p95 3.73 ms; workspace median was 11.38 ms and p95 20.60 ms. Full final-platform scale and production SLO certification were not run.
