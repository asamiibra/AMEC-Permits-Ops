# Accept and outputs

Human Proposal Accept creates the immutable `ProposalAcceptedRevision`. Controlled Proposal and Checklist artifacts are generated from that exact revision and pin the exact Dashboard Proposal Template and Checklist versions. Before Accept the workspace shows controlled unavailable output copy; after Accept it shows Proposal Download and Checklist Download links plus pinned version metadata.

Accept remains distinct from Client Acceptance, Contract execution, Project Activation, and external Authority submission. Existing Contract handoff consumes the accepted revision and remains a separate action.
