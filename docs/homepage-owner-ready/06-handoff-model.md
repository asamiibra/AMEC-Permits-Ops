# Handoff model

Cross-team handoffs are projected from `AssistantHandoff` or the relevant lifecycle state; they are not copied into a homepage task store. The item identifies the receiving team, business domain, reason, and exact context link. Owner sees the union. The receiving Business Development or Engineering view sees the handoff when its bounded visibility rule applies.

Completion remains domain-owned. AMEC Work has no generic “Mark complete” control; the CTA opens the proposal, contract, permit stage, issue, draft, or notification context that owns the transition.
